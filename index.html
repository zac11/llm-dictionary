Using the 3D library

  • Share a word — the Share button on any entry copies a link to it, or opens LinkedIn, X, Medium and email with the entry ready to paste.
  • Browse the shelf — drag to look around, scroll to zoom. Hovered books peek out of the shelf.
  • Pull a volume — click any dictionary book (A–B, C–D, …) and it slides off the shelf onto the reading desk.
  • Browse contents — volumes with more than 10 words paginate; jump with the printed page strip (1·2·3…) or turn with ← / →, PageUp / PageDown.
  • Read an entry — pick a word from the contents page; read entries with ← / → or the buttons.
  • Back to the glossary — the Contents button (or Esc) turns back to that volume's word list, so you can pick another word.
  • Search — type a term and press Enter: the right volume is pulled out and opened straight to that word. Press Esc to clear the search.
  • Put it back — press Esc or the close button and the book closes and returns to its shelf.

Words live as JSON files inside the dictionary/ folder grouped A–B, C–D, … Y–Z.
