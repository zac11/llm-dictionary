    Using the 3D dictionary

    • Explore — drag to rotate the shelf, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
    • Open a volume — click any 3D book (A–B, C–D, …) to list its words.
    • Read an entry — click a word for its definition, detail and citation.
    • Jump around — click a letter below the shelf or type in the search box.

    Words live as JSON files inside the dictionary/ folder grouped A–B, C–D, … Y–Z.
